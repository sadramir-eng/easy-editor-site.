Privacy Policy

Easy Editor

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Easy Editor is a creative editing project. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when using Easy Editor and its planned integrations.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features used, Easy Editor may process:

  • Creative assets selected or imported by users.
  • Search terms entered into the asset library.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate the application.
  • Authentication information required for optional third-party integrations.

3. Pinterest Integration

Easy Editor may use Pinterest's API to search and display Pinterest content. If this integration is enabled, Pinterest may process information according to its own Privacy Policy.

Easy Editor will only request permissions necessary for its features. Users will be informed when Pinterest authentication is required.

Easy Editor does not claim ownership of Pinterest content. Users should respect the rights and terms associated with each asset.

4. Storage and Security

Project files and imported assets may be stored locally or through services used by the application. Any authentication tokens must be handled securely and must not be exposed in public client-side code.

5. Sharing of Information

Easy Editor does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services when required to provide an enabled feature.

6. Children's Privacy

Easy Editor is intended to be developed and operated with appropriate privacy protections. Any age restrictions imposed by third-party services, including Pinterest, must be followed.

7. Third-Party Services

Third-party services have their own privacy policies and data practices. Users should review those policies before connecting their accounts.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Easy Editor develops. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information provided on the official Easy Editor website.
